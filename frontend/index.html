FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide free doorstep pickup?

Yes, we provide free doorstep pickup from your home or office address across India at your preferred time.

How long does standard delivery take?

Our express courier delivery takes approximately 3 to 5 working days depending on the destination country and customs clearance.

What documents are required for medicine shipping?

We require a valid doctor's prescription, the patient's passport copy, and the purchase invoice for the medicines.

How can I track my shipment?

You can track your shipment live using the waybill number in the dedicated Shipment Tracking section of our website.
